# License and provenance policy

A project-wide license has **not** been selected. Until one is explicitly chosen and added, do not assume that repository content is available for unrestricted reuse.

Before copying or adapting any upstream code:
- Identify the exact source repository, revision, file, and license.
- Preserve required copyright and license notices.
- Check whether modifications and distribution trigger source-sharing or other obligations.
- Keep third-party assets and binaries separate from original source where appropriate.

GT5-Unofficial and other GTNH components may have their own licenses. Never assume the entire modpack shares one license. This document is a workflow policy, not legal advice.
